Urchin QWERTY layers

Plain QWERTY with home-row mods, four layers held by the thumbs, and a player that walks through your real shortcuts press by press. Pick a layer to browse it, or pick a shortcut to watch how you'd press it.

    Mods on the home row

    ⌃ ⌥ ⌘ ⇧ under pinky, ring, middle, index on both hands. Hold one, then press a key on the other hand. Uses urob's timeless settings so fast typing doesn't trigger mods.

    Plain mods on NAV and NUM

    When a left thumb holds NAV or NUM, the left home row becomes ordinary modifier keys. ⌃⌥→ or ⌥3 never depends on timing.

    hjkl are the arrows

    Vim, Gmail, Linear and window managers already use h j k l, so the arrows live on those keys too.

    Numbers opposite the mods

    A right-hand numpad, 1-2-3 on top, held from the left thumb. Every ⌘/⌃/⌥ + number uses both hands.
